# OSIS Marketplace Cheatsheet

Quick reference for player-owned storefronts in **The Bazaar**, the OSIS marketplace for fashion, vehicles, homes, art, music, collectibles, and virtual services.

## Listing an item

1. **Own or license the asset.** You must have creator rights or written permission for any brands, likenesses, music, images, or third-party content.
2. **Choose a storefront.** Use your personal shop, a creator studio, or a partnered venue.
3. **Set the offer.** Provide clear title, description, price, currency (OSIS Coins or approved fiat equivalent), quantity, and any expiry.
4. **Apply age and content labels.** Mark mature, restricted, or region-limited items accurately.
5. **Confirm rights and authenticity.** Declare whether the item is original, licensed, or resold. Counterfeits, stolen assets, and rights-infringing listings are prohibited.
6. **Publish.** The listing enters discovery only after passing automated scans and, where required, a moderation hold.

## Buying an item

1. **Review the listing.** Check price, fees, refunds, creator attribution, and any usage limits or region restrictions.
2. **Verify your wallet or payment method.** Ensure sufficient OSIS Coins or a verified payment instrument.
3. **Confirm the purchase.** The final price, including all taxes and fees, must be shown before commitment.
4. **Receive the item.** The asset is transferred to your inventory with a transaction receipt and provenance record.
5. **Report problems.** Use the in-marketplace support flow for non-delivery, misdescription, or rights concerns.

## Selling and creator payouts

- Creators set prices; OSIS deducts platform and payment fees according to the published fee schedule.
- Payouts require a verified identity, tax form, or regional equivalent where legally required.
- Creator attribution is preserved on-chain or in the provenance record and survives resale.
- Payout schedules, minimums, currency conversion, and withdrawal terms are shown in the Creator Center.

## Provenance, resale, and takedown

- Every item has a provenance record: creator, owner, license terms, edition, and transaction history.
- Resale is allowed only when the item's license permits it.
- Creators can issue takedown requests for infringing copies; sellers can submit counter-notices.
- OSIS preserves evidence and suspends accounts that repeatedly infringe rights.

## Disputes and refunds

1. **Open a dispute** within the time window shown at purchase.
2. **Provide evidence:** receipt, screenshots, item ID, and a description of the issue.
3. **Human review** decides high-value or rights-related disputes.
4. **Refunds** return the purchase amount to the original payment method or OSIS Coins wallet, depending on the item type and region.

## Rules and compliance reminders

- No real-money wagering, unlicensed gambling, securities, or prohibited goods.
- No deceptive pricing, hidden fees, fake scarcity, or misleading promotions.
- Respect regional restrictions: some items may be unavailable in certain countries.
- Keep age-restricted or adult content behind appropriate labels and gates.
- Virtual currency and marketplace rules must not create hidden pay-to-win advantages in gameplay.

## Support

- **Marketplace support:** Help Center → Bazaar → Orders & Listings
- **Creator rights:** Help Center → Creator Rights & Takedowns
- **Safety reports:** Help Center → Safety & Moderation

> This cheatsheet is a quick reference, not legal, tax, or regulatory advice. Creator payouts, taxes, consumer rights, and marketplace rules depend on your jurisdiction and the current OSIS terms.
